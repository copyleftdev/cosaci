Why CosaCI
Your CI bill is renting compute you already own.

For years the industry accepted a strange bargain: pay a cloud provider by the minute to run code on machines you don't own, and receive a green checkmark you can't independently verify. That made sense when everyone was starting from scratch. Most teams aren't anymore. You have servers. Build machines sitting at 30% utilisation while the CI invoice climbs. CosaCI closes that gap — connect the infrastructure you already control, run jobs on it, and receive a cryptographic proof instead of a checkmark.

$0 runner cost after setup

Your hardware is bought and paid for. No per-minute billing, no monthly seats, no egress fees. The marginal cost of a CosaCI build is the electricity on a machine you were already running. Add capacity by connecting more machines you own — remove them when you need them back.

No vendor in the critical path

Every runner is infrastructure you control. No quota requests, no support tickets, no outage pages from a cloud provider you don't own. Your pipeline runs when your machines run, on a network you administer, with keys you hold.

A proof, not a checkmark

Cloud CI returns pass/fail. CosaCI returns a signed, content-addressed artifact anchored in a Merkle log. You can verify the result without trusting CosaCI, without trusting the runners, without trusting anyone. The math is the guarantee.
